1 Interpretation & Definitions

"App" refers to HayGo (com.haygo), a mobile web browser developed by Subhojit Haldar.

"We / Us / Our" refers to the developer and owner of HayGo — Subhojit Haldar, operating from West Bengal, India.

"You / User" refers to any individual who installs or uses HayGo.

"Personal Data" means information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.

"Website Data" means information associated with websites you visit, such as browsing history, downloaded files, cookies, and site storage, where supported by the browser.

2 Information & Permissions

HayGo is a web browser. Information may be processed to load websites and provide browser features such as back/forward navigation, refresh, home, device-view options, downloads, browsing history, and the floating menu. The app's Android package lists the permissions below. Actual use depends on the feature, Android version, and permissions granted by you.

  • INTERNET
    Allows the browser to connect to websites and online services you choose to visit.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
    Allows the app to check network connectivity status.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE
    Allows the app to access Wi-Fi connection state information.
  • CAMERA
    May be used when a website you visit requests camera access, subject to Android and browser permission controls.
  • RECORD_AUDIO
    May be used when a website requests microphone access, subject to permission controls.
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
    May provide approximate location if a website requests it and you grant access.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
    May provide precise location if a website requests it and you grant access.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS
    Allows notifications where the app uses them and you permit them.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
    Supports access to images selected or made available for browser features such as uploading files.
  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO
    Supports access to video media where needed for a user-selected browser action.
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO
    Supports access to audio media where needed for a user-selected browser action.
  • READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED
    Supports access to images or videos that you specifically select through Android's media picker.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC
    Allows biometric authentication if the app invokes a supported biometric feature.
  • VIBRATE
    Allows vibration for supported app interactions or feedback.
  • WAKE_LOCK
    Allows the app to keep the device awake for certain operations when needed.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS
    Allows changes to audio settings for supported media or website functions.
  • DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION
    An app-defined permission related to internal broadcast receiver security; it is not, by itself, a request to collect personal information.
Important: A permission being listed does not mean that the app continuously accesses that information. Camera, microphone, location, and media access should occur only when the relevant feature is used and permission is granted. Review Android permission settings to control access.

Websites you visit may independently receive information such as your IP address, browser/device details, and information you submit to them. Their collection practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

4 Local Data & Retention

Browsing history, download records, cookies, and website storage may be kept on your device by the app or its web-rendering component, depending on the features used and platform behavior.

  • You may be able to clear browsing history, downloads, cookies, or website data through the browser or Android settings, where those controls are available.
  • Downloaded files may remain on your device until you delete them.
  • Websites you visit may store or retain information on their own systems under their own policies.
  • This policy does not assert a server-side retention period for HayGo because no such collection or retention practice has been confirmed for this document.

5 Third-Party Websites

HayGo lets you access third-party websites. Those websites may use cookies, trackers, account systems, analytics, advertising, or other technologies and may collect information directly from you or your device.

We do not control the content, security, or privacy practices of third-party websites. Review the privacy policy and permissions of each website or service you use. The app's HTTPS-only restriction does not guarantee that a website itself is trustworthy.

7 Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell personal information as a stated purpose of HayGo. Information is sent to websites you choose to visit as necessary for those websites to operate, and those websites may process it under their own policies.

  • Websites you visit: Your browser requests and information you submit are sent to the destination website.
  • Legal requirements: Information may be disclosed if required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • Protection: Information may be handled as necessary to protect rights, safety, and security, subject to applicable law.

No third-party analytics, advertising SDK, or external data-sharing arrangement is asserted here because these have not been verified from the supplied screenshots.

8 Security

HayGo is designed to allow HTTPS websites and block HTTP websites, as described for the app. HTTPS helps protect data in transit between your device and a website, but it does not guarantee that a website is legitimate or safe.

No app, website, or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device updated, manage permissions carefully, and avoid submitting sensitive information to websites you do not trust.

9 Children's Privacy
